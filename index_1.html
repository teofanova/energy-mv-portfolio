Context & Purpose

This portfolio presents a documentation and reporting system I designed and built from scratch for an energy services company operating public lighting projects under Public-Private Partnership agreements across 40+ municipalities in Serbia.


The system was built to meet contractual M&V obligations — tracking guaranteed energy savings, comparing actual vs. projected consumption, and producing structured reports submitted to local government authorities as formal compliance documentation.

Data Integrity & Quality Control

The system was designed with built-in quality controls at every stage:

  • 1
    Contractor invoice verification Monthly invoices from subcontractors approved only when consumption data matches the meter point tracker. Mismatches flagged and followed up before payment approval.
  • 2
    Cross-document reconciliation Monthly totals in the meter point tracker must match data entered in the master savings report. Any discrepancy triggers a review of source electricity bills.
  • 3
    Deviation flagging Monthly comparison between projected and actual kWh is automatic. Significant deviations trigger a written note in the quarterly report with explanation.
  • 4
    Template version control All project reports produced from the same template version to ensure consistent legal language and formatting across all municipalities.
  • 5
    Pre-submission review Every report reviewed against source data before submission to municipality. No report goes out with unresolved discrepancies.
