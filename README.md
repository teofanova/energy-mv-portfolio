## About This Portfolio

This repository contains anonymized work samples demonstrating my experience in energy efficiency project coordination, Measurement & Verification (M&V) documentation, and data tracking systems.

All documents are portfolio demos — names, identifiers, and figures have been replaced with fictional data. Original work was produced for real PPP (Public-Private Partnership) energy efficiency projects across 40+ municipalities in Serbia.

---

## Documents

### 📊 Energy Savings Model (Excel)
A multi-sheet tracking model built from scratch to monitor guaranteed vs. actual energy savings across a 15-year guarantee period.

**What it demonstrates:**
- Linked assumptions, projections, and actuals across 9 interconnected sheets
- Monthly consumption tracking per meter point
- Automated deviation detection between projected and actual kWh
- 15-year operating cost and CO2 impact projections
- Energy price escalation scenarios

### 📄 Measurement & Verification Annual Report (Word)
Formal annual M&V report submitted to municipal authorities as contractual compliance documentation.

**What it demonstrates:**
- Template adaptation into project-specific compliance documents
- Structured reporting of achieved energy and cost savings
- Comparison of guaranteed vs. measured performance
- Environmental impact documentation (CO2 reduction)
- Professional formatting to government submission standards
